    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Tubular, flat plate or lithium: which battery should I buy?

    Tubular batteries last long and handle frequent, long power cuts, so they suit most homes. Flat plate batteries cost less and charge faster, good for short, occasional cuts. Lithium (LiFePO4) batteries cost more upfront but need no water top-up, have no acid fumes and last the longest.

    Which inverter type do I need?

    For homes, a pure sine wave inverter is safest for fridges, LED TVs and computers. Modified and square wave inverters cost less and are fine for fans and lights. With solar panels, choose off-grid (no grid needed), grid-tie (sells power to the grid) or hybrid (solar + battery + grid).

    How long does an inverter battery last?

    A well-maintained tubular battery usually lasts around 4–6 years, and a flat plate battery around 2–4 years. Regular water top-up and avoiding full discharge help a lot.

    Do I need a stabilizer for my AC or fridge?

    If your area has voltage ups and downs, yes. A stabilizer protects the compressor from damage. Tell us your appliance and we'll suggest the right rating.

    Do you take my old battery?

    Yes. We give exchange value for old batteries, adjusted against your new purchase. The value depends on the battery's size and weight.
