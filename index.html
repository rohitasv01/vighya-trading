    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Tubular or flat plate: which battery should I buy?

    Tubular batteries last longer and handle frequent, long power cuts better, so they are the best choice for most homes. Flat plate batteries cost less and charge faster, which suits areas with short, occasional cuts.

    How long does an inverter battery last?

    A well-maintained tubular battery usually lasts around 4–6 years, and a flat plate battery around 2–4 years. Regular water top-up and avoiding full discharge help a lot.

    How often should I top up distilled water?

    Check the level every 2–3 months, more often in summer or with long daily cuts. Use only distilled water and keep the level between the min and max marks.

    Do you take my old battery?

    Yes. We give exchange value for old inverter batteries, which is adjusted against your new purchase. Value depends on the battery's size and weight.

    What size inverter do I need?

    Add up the watts of everything you want to run and divide by 0.8 to get the VA rating. Our backup calculator does this for you, or just call us.
